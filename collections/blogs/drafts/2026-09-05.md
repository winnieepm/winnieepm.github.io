The first DH development project I worked on died sometime in the middle of spring 2025 after its domain expired. _Coasts in Crisis_ was a digital art exhibit created in 2020–2021 featuring pieces produced by local artists in the Caribbean during the devastating 2017 hurricane season. Its main goal was creating an online footprint of the event that gathered 7 artists and showcased their work at the University of Virginia on 2019. Though I failed my goal of migrating the live project into a static site generator.

The original website was designed by me and co-developed with Elise Foote using Drupal 7. It was deployed through Reclaim Hosting under the `coastsincrisis.net` domain name during my first and second year of graduate school.[^1] I maintained and backed up the website in Drupal until its domain expired. As it happens with so many digital projects, once grant-funding for its development ended, project team members moved on to other responsibilities, some graduated, and the project was abandoned. Drupal's constant security updates and the release of Drupal 10 as a major platform modernization made project maintenance tedious and unsustainable, and the domain name expired before the site was ever migrated to a different platform.
