I like difficult questions. I'm a PhD-trained technologist, researcher, and educator with experience in higher education institutions, organizations, and journals.

Technologist, researcher, and educator specializing in digital humanities and Caribbean cultural studies from a literary framing. My current research identifies grassroots sovereign practices of social transformation and knowledge creation in Puerto Rican science fiction to explore their significance for post-capitalist modes of scholarship. 

I help small teams manage and build digital projects, and my current research project analyzes the material effects of technology infrastructures in the Caribbean. My work combines project management, critical digital pedagogy, and web development.
