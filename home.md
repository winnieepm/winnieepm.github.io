I like difficult questions and fuzzy data.

I'm a technologist, researcher, and educator specializing in digital humanities and Caribbean cultural studies from a literary framing. I have experience working in higher education institutions, professional organizations, and journals. I enjoy helping small teams manage, maintain, and build digital projects. My work combines project management, critical digital pedagogy, and web development.

My current research identifies grassroots sovereign practices of social transformation and knowledge creation in Puerto Rican science fiction to explore their significance for post-capitalist modes of scholarship. I live obsessed with infrastructures.
